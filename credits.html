Illustrating the Rookie Classic

The cover shows the moment two training partners spot the sign-up sheet. The other two illustrations follow the athlete from the neighborhood weight room to the community hall on show night. These scenes were created for this game with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool. The game draws the athletes, judges, and entry card in the browser.

Gym noticeboard

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a polished wide 16:9 landscape cover illustration for an independent sports narrative game about a first-time local bodybuilding show. An ordinary neighborhood gym on a late afternoon, depicted in a beautifully hand-inked vintage sports editorial illustration with screenprint grain, clean silhouettes, warm muted orange and navy, muted teal, ivory paper highlights. A very specific human moment: within the RIGHT half of frame, an adult recreational lifter with short dark wavy hair, tan skin, ivory sleeveless top, orange gym shorts and a small duffel looks at the sign-up sheet pinned to the gym's cork noticeboard. Beside them a training partner in a teal T-shirt points casually at the blank lines on the sheet. A small trophy pictogram on the sheet but NO readable lettering. Large factory windows cast warm angular sunlight across brick, rubber flooring and a bench. The LEFT 42 percent is dark navy gym architecture and understated shadow, suitable for white HTML title text to be added later. Show a working, welcoming community gym on a human scale. Beautiful graphic composition, expressive natural postures, stylized but anatomically credible adults, a compelling illustrated game cover. No lettering, no watermarks, no interface, no fantasy or outer space.

Training room

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Wide 3:2 background painting for the training scene of a neighborhood bodybuilding game. The camera faces the back wall straight on at chest height. A real, well-used community weight room with soft morning light from a bank of high rectangular windows overlooking the tops of brick buildings and a leafy tree on an ordinary street. A rack of iron dumbbells and a folded towel at the far left, weight plates stored on a peg and a flat bench at the far right. The central half is completely unobstructed for a game character to stand in later. A simple exercise mat and a clean level dark rubber floor occupy the bottom quarter. Refined hand-inked sports comic, restrained screenprint grain, flat muted blue-green and charcoal midtones, warm apricot light, softly lit center that will contrast with a human figure in an ivory tank and orange shorts. Believable perspective and equipment. Empty room, no people or human reflections, no logos, no writing, no interface or borders.

Community hall stage

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Very wide horizontal 2.4:1 panorama of an EMPTY stage for a small community gym's first-time bodybuilding show, seen straight on from the audience. A broad flat low wooden platform spans the full bottom quarter of the picture; leave the entire platform clear for three athlete characters to be composited. Behind it are dark plum folded theatre curtains, simple painted brick at either side, warm amber stage lights on two portable lighting stands, an understated cream pennant with a small simple trophy silhouette high at the back. At the extreme left just offstage, one modest gold cup on a little awards table. Quietly exciting local recreation hall, not a big professional arena. Tasteful hand-inked vintage sports comic illustration, subtle screenprint grain, graphic silhouettes and warm ochre highlights in a charcoal, dusty teal and plum palette. No people on stage, no crowds blocking the foreground, no text, no logos, no watermark.

Type and sound

The titles use Barlow Condensed and the body text uses DM Sans. Both typefaces are bundled with the game under the SIL Open Font License. The optional game sounds are short tones synthesized in the browser.
