Illustrating Vela

The cover and two arena environments were created for this game with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool. The game draws the athletes, their changing muscle shapes, the judges, and the competition ticket in the browser.

Cover illustration

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create the original full-bleed key art for a polished independent sci-fi sports adventure game, 1792x1024 widescreen landscape cover. Retro-futurist screenprinted sports travel poster meets a beautifully inked European science-fiction comic, hand-drawn silhouettes, very deliberate high-contrast spot-color shapes, restrained halftone and light paper grain, beautiful sharp illustrative detail, visually rich and playful. On the RIGHT side, well within frame, a single adult human athlete in a plain ivory sleeveless sports top, warm orange gym shorts with a dark side stripe, teal wrist wraps, white sneakers, short wavy dark hair, carrying a small gym bag, seen three-quarter from behind. The athlete has just arrived on a spectacular otherworldly rocky planet. They look across curving red clay terraces to an immense open-air round bodybuilding arena with an upright circular portal on its stage. A vivid apricot sun and a smaller pale periwinkle moon float in an inky dark purple sky. Monumental architecture, lavender mesas and a few delicate meteor lines, dusk atmosphere. Art-directed composition: the athlete and arena and brightest sunset are within the RIGHT 58% of the canvas, the LEFT 42% is fairly dark open sky and low dark ridges where large white HTML lettering will be overlaid later. Art still extends naturally edge to edge. Make an unforgettable game cover with a sense of scale and wonder. No text, no logo, no numbers, no watermarks, no typography, no UI, no border.

Training room

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. A beautifully illustrated BACKGROUND ONLY for a side-on sports video game, empty training room in a retrofuturist alien arena. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Camera faces the back wall straight on, from the athlete's chest height. The open middle half of the frame is completely clear for a full-body game character to be placed later. Floor begins at 72% image height, with a subtle broad illuminated oval exercise mat in the center near the bottom. On both extreme sides only: low racks of iron weights, a bench, hangers, rings on cables, athletic details. Beyond a huge arched observation window, see purple alien mountains, small pale moon, orange dusk through lavender haze. Cool desaturated indigo, teal and violet midtones in the central interior so a brightly colored character will read well. Warm peach slivers of side light, a striking but quiet sense of place. High craft, retro-futurist sports travel-poster lithograph, detailed inked European science-fiction comic, soft halftone and light paper grain, deliberate clean perspective, substantial shapes. No persons, no bodies, no humanoid reflections, no silhouettes of people, no text, no numbers, no watermarks, no signs, no interface or borders.

Competition stage

Open the illustration

Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Original cinematic environment background art for the final of a retro sci-fi bodybuilding game. A very wide landscape panorama, imagined as a painted screenprinted European sci-fi sports comic, amber rim lighting in a plum and deep indigo night, restrained fine halftone, confident graphic silhouettes, squarely front-facing view from the judges' box. A WIDE LEVEL stage spans the entire lower quarter of frame, flat dark ochre stage flooring, no steps or pedestals on this platform: three full-body game sprites will stand in a horizontal line in front of this background. At center back a tall gold circular PORTAL, its inside showing a small blue and green Earth in a deep aqua starfield. Surrounding stone arches curve gently around the arena; thousands of very small dots of spectators high and far behind the unoccupied stage. Two pale moons. A few warm violet and amber theatrical beams from overhead. Frame is cinematic but not chaotic: strongest detail around edges of arena and portal, foreground empty. Feel of a real championship final with wonder and scale. No people or figures on the stage, no close spectators, no text, no logos, no scoreboards, no letters or numbers, no interface, no watermark.

Type and sound

The titles use Barlow Condensed and the body text uses DM Sans. Both typefaces are bundled with the game under the SIL Open Font License. The optional game sounds are short tones synthesized in the browser.
